How to play

The scorecard

Upper section

Ones through Sixes. Score the sum of all dice showing that number. Reach 63 or more across the six upper boxes to earn the 35-point upper bonus.

Lower section

  • Three of a Kind — three or more alike: sum of all five dice.
  • Four of a Kind — four or more alike: sum of all five dice.
  • Full House — three of one number and two of another: 25.
  • Small Straight — four dice in sequence: 30.
  • Large Straight — five dice in sequence: 40.
  • Yahtzee — five of a kind: 50.
  • Chance — sum of all five dice.

Bonuses

Upper bonus. 35 points when the upper section totals 63 or more.

Yahtzee bonus. After the Yahtzee box has been scored for 50, every extra five-of-a-kind is worth +100. A five-of-a-kind also counts as a full house (joker rule), and you may bank it in any open box.

You get three rolls each turn. Hold any dice between rolls, then bank the result in one open box. You may stop and bank after any roll.

The opponent

The computer does not guess. It enumerates every distinct outcome of every remaining roll, solves each turn backwards by dynamic programming, and scores into the box that maximises its expected final total. The value of the boxes it has left comes from a model calibrated with tens of thousands of seeded self-play games.

It plays close to the theoretical optimum of 254.59 points.
